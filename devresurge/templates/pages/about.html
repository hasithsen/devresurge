# DevResurge

DevResurge is a small, opinionated home for the tech crowd. Profiles read like a README — handle, headline, stack, experience, projects, badges, peer endorsements. Nothing more, nothing less.

## complementary to LinkedIn

Keep LinkedIn for your career network, jobs, and company graph. Use DevResurge for technical signal recruiters and collaborators actually need: what you ship, what you can prove (quizzes), who vouches for your stack, and what you’re open to (work, collaborate, mentor). Level up via ~/learn roadmaps — foundations, DSA, system design, DevOps, and more — then verify with badges. Share your public /u/handle/map/ as network proof — peers, mutuals, and open-to intents — next to LinkedIn.

Link both. Your DevResurge page can deep-link to LinkedIn; your LinkedIn featured section can point here. Two surfaces, one identity — no feed to manage.

## why?

Most portfolio sites drown you in plugins. LinkedIn buries signal under noise. We wanted something fast, accessible, and a little nostalgic — a terminal you can publish.
